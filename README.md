# Alepotrypa Lovers: Tao’s EQT on a Budget Laptop

Like complementing lovers, Mace4 can show non-existence 
of a proof by a counter example, while Prover9 can exhibit 
a specimen of a proof for a formalizable problem. We wrote 
small 100% Prolog versions that can attack a majority of 
Tao’s EQT problems on a budget laptop.

Our 100% Prolog code makes heavy use of hashtables, in their 
backtracking and non-backtracking form. The final David 
against Goliath benchmark, where we compare with LLM results 
from SAIR, is in favor of our David with an average of 20% 
more accuracy and 200–400x more speed.
